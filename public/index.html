    How it works: your app writes each request to logcat with a tiny debug-only logger, and droidpanel shows it here. Works with HTTPS, no certificates or proxy needed.

    Flutter (Dio): copy droidpanel_net.dart into your app, then:

    import 'package:flutter/foundation.dart';

if (kDebugMode) dio.interceptors.add(DroidpanelNetLog());

    Native Android (OkHttp / Retrofit): copy DroidpanelNet.kt, then:

    OkHttpClient.Builder()
    .apply { if (BuildConfig.DEBUG) addNetworkInterceptor(droidpanel.DroidpanelNet()) }
    .build()

    Authorization and cookie headers are masked. Bodies over 64 KB are truncated. Requests made before you press Capture also appear if they are still in the logcat buffer.

    Press Capture, then use your app. No requests showing? Click Setup.
